1""" 

2This file contains the prompt templates for the therapeutic training task. 

3Created by Alexey Obukhov, alexey.obukhov@hotmail.com, 2025 Dresden 

4 

5Purpose: 

6The `prompt_templates` dictionary provides various templates for generating therapeutic responses. These templates 

7are designed to guide the language model in generating relevant and helpful responses during a therapy session. 

8Each template is tailored to a specific therapeutic technique or approach, ensuring that the generated responses 

9are appropriate and effective. 

10 

11Functionality: 

12General Therapeutic Approaches: 

131. Question: Generate a relevant and helpful open-ended question for the user. 

142. Restatement or Paraphrasing: Restate or paraphrase the user's statement in a concise and empathetic way. 

153. Reflection of feelings: Identify and reflect the user's feelings expressed in their statement. 

164. Self-disclosure: Share a brief personal experience if it feels genuinely helpful and relevant to the user's situation. 

175. Affirmation and Reassurance: Offer a statement of affirmation and reassurance. 

186. Providing Suggestions: Offer a gentle and relevant suggestion or coping strategy. 

197. Information: Provide relevant psychoeducational information in a concise, empathetic, and easy-to-understand way. 

208. Others: Respond to the user in a helpful, supportive, and deeply empathetic way. 

219. Empathy and Validation: Show deep empathy and validate the user's feelings. 

22 

23Specific Therapeutic Modalities: 

2410. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Apply principles of CBT in a friendly and collaborative way. 

2511. Mindfulness and Relaxation: Guide the user through mindfulness and relaxation techniques. 

2612. Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT): Use SFBT techniques in a positive and empowering way. 

2713. Motivational Interviewing: Use Motivational Interviewing techniques in a non-judgmental and collaborative way. 

2814. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT): Apply principles of ACT in a compassionate and supportive way. 

2915. Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT): Use DBT techniques in a supportive and educational way. 

30 

31Topic-Specific Templates: 

3216. Relationship Issues: Address relationship dynamics, communication issues, or relationship decisions. 

3317. Heartbreak: Provide support for emotional pain following relationship loss or difficulty. 

3418. Workplace Trauma: Offer validation and empowerment strategies for workplace abuse or toxic environments. 

3519. Trauma: Provide trauma-informed support with emphasis on safety and stabilization. 

3620. Grief and Loss: Offer compassionate validation for those navigating the complex emotions of grief. 

3721. Depression: Validate experiences and offer realistic encouragement for depressive symptoms. 

3822. Anxiety: Provide calm validation and practical support for worry, fear, and physical anxiety symptoms. 

3923. Crisis Support: Deliver immediate emotional support and stabilization for those in urgent distress. 

40 

41Template Structure: 

42Each template follows a consistent pattern designed to: 

43- Establish the therapeutic context 

44- Provide guidance on appropriate response approach 

45- List specific elements to include in the response 

46- Maintain a warm, supportive tone 

47- Focus on the user's needs and experiences 

48 

49Confidence-Based Selection: 

50The system uses confidence scoring to select the most appropriate template based on: 

51- Topic relevance (how well the detected topic matches the template's focus) 

52- Detected emotional tones in the user's message 

53- Urgency signals that may require immediate support 

54- Pattern recognition for specific situations (e.g., relationship breakups with depression) 

55 

56Fallback Mechanisms: 

57- When confidence is low, the system defaults to general empathy and validation approaches 

58- Special handling for urgent or crisis situations 

59- Topic-specific fallbacks for common issues like relationship problems or depression 

60 

61Disclaimer: 

62The AI-generated responses using these templates are not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. 

63Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. 

64Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read or generated by an AI model. 

65If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or emergency services immediately. 

66 

67Ethical Precautions: 

681. The AI-generated responses should be used to supplement, not replace, the guidance of a licensed therapist or counselor. 

692. Always prioritize the user's safety and well-being. If the user expresses thoughts of self-harm or harm to others, seek immediate professional help. 

703. Maintain confidentiality and privacy of the user's information. 

714. Use the AI-generated responses as a tool to enhance therapeutic interactions, not as a standalone solution. 

725. Be mindful of cultural, gender, and individual differences in how people experience and express emotional distress. 

73 

74Implementation Notes: 

75- Template selection uses enhanced topic detection with confidence scoring 

76- Response generation adapts based on confidence levels, with fallback mechanisms 

77- Special handling for typos and misspellings in user inputs 

78- Composite topic detection for more nuanced understanding of user needs 

79

81 from psy-supabase.utilities.templates.therapeutic_prompt import prompt_templates
